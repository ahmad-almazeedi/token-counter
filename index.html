Free online character counter, word counter, and token counter

iLostCount is a plain text box that keeps a running count of everything above it. Paste a draft, a prompt, or a bio into the canvas at the top of this page and the characters, words, and AI tokens update as you type. There is no button to press, no upload, and no account. The counting runs inside your browser, so your text never leaves your device.

What it counts

Characters
Every letter, digit, space, and punctuation mark, ignoring blank space at the very start and end. Useful for the hard limits on social posts, meta descriptions, SMS messages, and application forms. A raw character count that keeps outer whitespace is available in the filter menu.
Words
Runs of text separated by whitespace, counted the way an essay, article, or word limit expects.
Lines and paragraphs
How many lines your text occupies and how many blocks it is split into, handy for formatting checks and for anything with a per line limit.
Tokens
An estimate of how many tokens an AI model such as Claude or GPT would read your text as. English averages roughly four characters per token, so a 1,000 character prompt is usually somewhere near 250 tokens. Use it to keep prompts inside a context window or to sanity check API cost before you send a request.
Speaking time
How long the text takes to read aloud at a normal presentation pace of 150 words per minute. Written a talk, a video script, or a voiceover? This tells you whether it fits the slot.

Compare two texts side by side

The compare button opens a second canvas next to the first. Each side is counted on its own and the difference between them appears beside every count, which makes it quick to check an edit against the original, fit a rewrite to the same length, or see how much a shorter prompt saves you. A Markdown preview button renders either side as formatted text without changing what is counted.

Good to know

How do I count characters in a text online?

Paste or type into the box at the top of this page. The character count updates as you type, with nothing to press and nothing to install.

Does the character count include spaces?

Yes. The main count includes spaces and punctuation but ignores blank space at the very start and end of your text. For the exact raw length including that outer whitespace, turn on Characters (raw) in the filter menu.

What is a token, and how is the token count calculated?

A token is the unit AI models use to read text, usually a short word or a piece of a longer one. This page estimates the count from your text's length and word count. The exact number varies by model, so treat it as a close approximation rather than a billing figure.

How many words is a 5 minute speech?

At about 150 words per minute, roughly 750 words. The speaking time counter applies that same pace to your text, so you can trim a script until it fits.

Is my text stored or sent anywhere?

No. All counting runs locally in your browser. Your text is never sent to a server, never stored, and there is no sign up. The site records cookieless page view counts only.
